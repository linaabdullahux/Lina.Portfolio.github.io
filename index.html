Graduation Project — Family Visitation Platform

Shaml+ (شمل+)

Shaml+ is the first step of an integrated system designed following a User-Centered Design methodology — a unified digital platform that brings together every party involved (parents, children, and supervisors) to improve the experience of family visits for children in cases of parental separation.

During this phase, we studied the problem in depth, conducted user research, analyzed needs, and built and tested early-stage designs, laying the groundwork for the project's next phase.
